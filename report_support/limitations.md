# Limitations to report explicitly

1. **Dataset overlap:** one normalised exact test item (#1545) appears twice in training; 131/152/43 goals repeat across train-validation/train-test/validation-test. No exact ordered or unordered casefolded candidate-pair overlap occurs in train-validation. Repeated goals do not necessarily mean identical problems, but item independence is imperfect. Historical results were not silently recomputed on a new split.
2. **Exposed test set:** historical outputs and qualitative examples were already inspected before this audit. New engineering verification cannot restore an unseen benchmark; no test-directed model selection was performed here.
3. **Directional alignment:** SequenceMatcher tie-breaking makes tags change for 755 training examples after raw candidate swaps. Network equivariance applies to encoded pairs only. Canonical alignment is tested but remains opt-in future work, without historical accuracy claims.
4. **Truncation:** 54 training, 7 validation and 2 test examples lose their distinguishing token sequence under prefix truncation. One additional training pair is identical under full tokenisation. This prevents meaningful discrimination; argmax tie-breaking favours option 1. Changes centred on differing spans must be validated in a separate study.
5. **Uncertainty:** three seeds share one validation split. Hyperparameter selection uses one seed per grid candidate. Small differences do not establish causal component benefits. CIs describe representative seeds, not mean accuracy; unadjusted paired p-values require multiplicity disclosure.
6. **Ablation confounds:** cross-module removal reduces capacity, mean pooling removes two mechanisms, vanilla is a bundle, MLM changes compute, and common seed values do not match initial weights across architectures. No experiment removes all attention from DACT.
7. **Pretraining comparisons:** different model sizes, tokenizers, objectives and exposure prevent isolating world knowledge. Historical Hub revisions were not retained, and baseline pretraining contamination is unknown.
8. **Attention interpretation:** the tag prior already favours differences at initialisation. Head-averaged slices hide variation and omit keys/CLS sink. Confidence-selected examples are not representative. No causal attribution or calibrated confidence is established.
9. **Artifact loss:** original A100 weights/predictions are absent in this clone. Aggregate metrics are supported by logs and saved outputs but cannot recreate per-item decisions. New replication artifacts do not retroactively verify old significance tests.
10. **Reproduction:** the audit verifies local Windows GPU execution and development notebook execution. A new clean Colab full run, Drive mounting and the modified B3/B4 full pathways remain to be verified. The historical executed notebook remains the source of full baseline/ablation evidence.
11. **Submission:** the ACL report and actual team contribution statement are still to be written. Main content must stay within six pages; references/contributions are excluded. Include only Group 69 in the author field and use ACL final mode. Supporting notes are not a finished report.
